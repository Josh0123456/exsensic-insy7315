## Part 1 to final design and adoption

Task 1 individual marking feedback was not available for verification; this section compares the submitted Part 1 baseline with the final implementation. The comparison is limited to baseline descriptions preserved in CONTRACTS §12 and architecture §8; the original submission itself remains unavailable.

The final lifecycle has four statuses; rejection is Cancelled with a reason. Rescheduling an applicable booking returns it to Requested, so approval is repeated. Upcoming and Past are presentation filters. The wizard is slot first, requirements second. Azure is the final hosting platform. MVC calls a separate API using shared Contracts; Web does not enforce qualification or overlap rules. Notifications are in-app; email must not be promised. These are explicit final decisions, not rewritten original requirements.

Adoption: demonstrate the client journey with an authorised demo account; give clients the illustrated guide; practise reading Requested versus Confirmed; explain SAST and cancellation restrictions; let staff/admin owners deliver role training; record support contacts, questions and defects; repeat acceptance after fixes. The client guide supports handover by describing actions, outcomes and recovery rather than implementation details.
