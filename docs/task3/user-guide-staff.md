# User guide: staff

For Exsensic staff who deliver the booked work. You can see only the bookings assigned to you. You can't see other people's bookings, and you can't confirm, reject or cancel a booking: the admin team does that.

Screenshots are placeholders, to be captured from staging after the Task 3 deployment.

Your sign-in details are given to you privately by an administrator. They are never written in this guide, in the repository, or in chat.

## 1. Sign in

1. Open the Exsensic site and select **Sign in** in the navigation bar.
2. Enter your **Email address** and **Password**, then select **Sign in**.
3. You land on **My schedule**.

If sign-in fails, the message is deliberately the same whether the email or the password is wrong. After five wrong tries in a row, sign-in is paused for 15 minutes, even if you then type the right password. Wait and try again, or ask an administrator.

Staff accounts can't be created on the Register page. An administrator creates your account. If you can't sign in at all, your account may have been deactivated; contact an administrator.

Your session ends after 30 minutes. You'll be asked to sign in again.

## 2. Your weekly schedule

1. Select **My schedule** in the navigation bar.
2. The page shows one week, Monday to Sunday, with the dates under the heading. Times are South African time (SAST).
3. Work is grouped by day. Today has a **Today** label.
4. Each card shows the client's company, the booking reference, the status, the service, the date, the time and, once assigned, your name.
5. Use **Previous week**, **This week** and **Next week** to move around.
6. Select **View booking** on a card to open it.

Only confirmed and completed work assigned to you appears. A booking that is still waiting for approval, or has been cancelled, is not on your schedule. If a week is empty you'll see **Nothing scheduled this week**.

> **Figure 2.1** Weekly schedule with day groups and the week buttons.

## 3. Booking details

Select **View booking** to see everything you need for the job:

- **Booking overview**: reference, service, date, time (SAST), status, assigned staff, the client's company and contact details (name, email and phone).
- **Project requirements**: what the client asked for. See section 4.
- **Booking history**: each change with the date, who made it and any note, oldest first.
- **Cancellation or rejection reason**: shown only if the booking was cancelled.

Use **Back to schedule** to return.

> **Figure 3.1** Booking details with the overview and history.

## 4. Client requirements

The **Project requirements** panel lists the client's answers to the questions for the service they booked. For example, a photoshoot lists the type of shoot, the location, how many items and a description.

- Read it before the job. If something is unclear, ask an administrator to check with the client.
- If the client gave no details you'll see **No requirement details were supplied.**
- You can't change the requirements. If they have to change, the client reschedules or cancels, or an administrator helps.

Client details are for delivering this booking only. Don't copy them out of the system, into chat or into other documents.

> **Figure 4.1** Project requirements panel.

## 5. Mark a booking as completed

1. Open the booking after the work has been delivered.
2. Select **Mark as completed**.
3. In the box **Mark this work as completed?**, check the work has been delivered and select **Mark as completed**.
4. A green message says **The booking has been marked as completed.** The status changes to **Completed** and the client is notified.

Things to know:

- The button appears only for a **Confirmed** booking **after its start time**. Before the start, there is no button, because the work can't have been delivered yet.
- Marking a booking as completed can't be undone. Check it is the right booking and that the work is finished.
- Completed bookings stay on your schedule so you can look back.

> **Figure 5.1** Mark as completed button and confirmation box.

## 6. Notifications

The **Notifications** link in the navigation bar shows a number when you have unread updates.

1. Open **Notifications**. New items are marked **New** and shown in bold.
2. Select **View booking** to open the booking the update is about.
3. Select **Mark as read** when you've dealt with it.

You are told when:

- you are **assigned** to a booking;
- a booking you were assigned to is **moved to a new time**. It then goes back to waiting for approval, so check your schedule before you plan around it;
- a booking you were assigned to is **cancelled**.

## 7. Troubleshooting

### What each status means

| Status | What it means for you | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Requested** | The client asked for this time and an administrator has not approved it yet. | Nothing. It doesn't appear on your schedule. |
| **Confirmed** | Approved, and you are assigned. | Prepare for the job. After the start time you can mark it as completed. |
| **Completed** | The work was delivered and recorded. | Nothing. It is final and stays on your schedule. |
| **Cancelled** | The client or an administrator cancelled it, or it was rejected. The reason is on the booking. | Nothing. It is final and leaves your schedule. You'll get a notification if you were assigned. |

A confirmed booking that the client or an administrator moves to another time goes back to **Requested**. It leaves your schedule until an administrator approves the new time again.

### Common problems

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **There is no "Mark as completed" button** | The booking is not **Confirmed**, or the start time has not passed yet. | Check the status and the start time (SAST). If it is Confirmed and the time has passed, reload the page. If there is still no button, tell an administrator. |
| **A booking disappeared from my schedule** | It was cancelled, or moved to a new time and is waiting for approval again. | Open **Notifications** and read the update. Ask an administrator if you are not sure. |
| **A booking is at a different time than yesterday** | It was rescheduled and approved again. | Use the new date and time shown on the booking. |
| **Review the latest booking** (a page with a **Reload booking** button) | The booking changed while you were looking at it, for example an administrator cancelled it just before you pressed the button. Nothing was saved. | Select **Reload booking**, read the latest status, then decide what to do. |
| **Someone else changed this record. Please refresh and review it before trying again.** | Same as above: your page was out of date. | Reload the page and check the booking before you try again. |
| **That action is no longer available for this booking. Please refresh the page.** | The booking is no longer in a state where that action is allowed, for example it is already Completed or was cancelled. | Reload the page and check the status. |
| **The requested item could not be found.** | The booking doesn't exist, or it is not assigned to you. You can only open your own bookings. | Go back to **My schedule** and open the booking from there. |
| **You do not have permission to do that.** | You tried to open a page for another role, such as the admin pages. | Use only the staff pages. Contact an administrator if you think you need access. |
| **Too many requests. Please wait before trying again.** | You pressed buttons too fast, or tried to sign in too many times. | Wait a minute (or the number of seconds on the page) and try again. |
| **Something went wrong with the service. Please try again later.** | A fault on our side. Nothing you did caused it. | Try again in a few minutes. If it continues, tell an administrator and give the time it happened. |
| **This online service is not available yet** | The service can't be reached at the moment. No request was sent. | Try again later. |

### Unavailable slots, cut-off times and errors

Staff don't book, move or cancel time slots, so you won't normally see these messages. You may hear about them from clients:

- **"That time slot is no longer available."** The client chose a time that someone else took first, or that the administrators blocked. The client picks another time. It has no effect on your schedule.
- **"The cancellation deadline has passed."** A client can't cancel a confirmed booking within 24 hours of its start, because staff have already prepared. They are asked to contact the team. Only an administrator can cancel at that point. If an administrator cancels, you'll be notified.
- **Messages about checking highlighted fields** appear on forms. The only form you use is the one-button **Mark as completed**, so you shouldn't see these.

If a problem isn't listed here, note the booking reference, what you pressed and the time (SAST), and tell an administrator.

## 8. Signing out safely

1. Select **Sign out** in the navigation bar when you finish, especially on a shared computer.
2. Close the browser on a shared or public computer.
3. If you think someone else has used your account, tell an administrator straight away so your password can be changed.
